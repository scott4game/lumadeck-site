Support

Connect to your Mac

Connect both devices to the same trusted Wi-Fi. On the Mac, turn on Screen Sharing and Remote Login in System Settings → General → Sharing. Allow your local user in both services. Select the computer in LumaDeck and use its Mac login.

Cannot connect?

Allow Local Network access for LumaDeck on the iPhone or iPad. Check that the Mac is awake and that your account may use Remote Login. After a host key change, verify the fingerprint directly on your Mac before pairing again.

Using the controls

Open the session toolbar for the keyboard, shortcuts and rotation. Edit a saved computer to copy its configuration without the password.

Contact support

Contact the LumaDeck maintainer on GitHub for help, bug reports or feature requests. Reports are public: do not include passwords, SSH keys, IP addresses or personal screenshots.
